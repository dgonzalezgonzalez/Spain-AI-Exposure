# Sources, provenance, and reuse

This release combines the coauthor archive `last_scripts_code_only.zip`, the `main` preparation/rendering corrections, the Jev occupation branch (commit `b2f741d`), and retained strict-SDID table/code changes inspected at commit `90d4167`. Publication membership is determined solely by the manuscript and assets downloaded from Prism on 7 October 2026, not by the presence of an older generated file. Exact snapshot hashes appear in the input and reference manifests. Prior `main` is preserved by tag `archive/pre-replication-main-2026-10-07`; experimental branches are retained for history.

## Administrative and survey statistics

- **SEPE (2026), Información del mercado de trabajo por ocupación.** [Provider](https://www.sepe.es/HomeSepe/que-es-observatorio/informacion-mt-por-ocupacion.html). CNO-2011 four-digit monthly reports, January 2021–March 2026. Author scraping/reshaping retains report URLs. [Reuse notice](https://www.sepe.es/HomeSepe/en/mas-informacion-contacto/aviso-legal.html) governs public-sector reuse, source attribution, and identification of modifications. Counts are public aggregate tabulations. The age/province May 2024 files are author backcasts, not new provider observations.
- **INE, CNO-2011 explanatory notes.** [Original PDF](https://ine.es/daco/daco42/clasificaciones/cno11_notas.pdf). Source for Spanish occupation descriptions; author parsing combines definitions, tasks, included examples and exclusions, with the documented section-length limits.
- **INE, EPA table 65134.** [CSV endpoint](https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/files/t/csv_bdsc/65134.csv). Occupation-by-sex employment used for CNO2 female employment shares and their specified baseline periods.
- **INE, EPA public-use microdata, 2021Q1–2026Q1.** [Provider download page](https://www.ine.es/prodyser/micro_epa.htm). The extract `epa_unemployment_microdata_weights.csv.gz` retains only quarter, activity classification `AOI` (05/06), and `FACTOREL` for 143,554 unemployed survey records. No household, person, geographic or employment identifiers are included. Summing original weights reproduces the exact microdata vintage used by the manuscript's source check; the current published aggregate table is a different vintage. All 21 original download URLs, ZIP SHA-256 hashes, member names and retained record counts are in `docs/epa_microdata_sources.json`. INE reuse terms require source attribution and identification of author transformations.

## Occupational measures and descriptions

- **Massenkoff, Maxim, and Peter McCrory (2026), Labor market impacts of AI: A new measure and early evidence.** [Research page](https://www.anthropic.com/research/labor-market-impacts); [occupation exposure CSV](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Anthropic/EconomicIndex/raw/main/labor_market_impacts/job_exposure.csv). Archived 756 occupations. The [dataset README](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Anthropic/EconomicIndex/blob/main/README.md) states CC-BY for released data and MIT for code, despite its generic metadata license field. Preserve attribution; this package's mappings are author modifications.
- **Anthropic Economic Index, 26 June 2026 release, Cadences.** [Frozen release URL](https://economic-research.anthropic.com/releases/econ-index/release-2026-06-26.zip). The supplied ZIP contains `aei_claude_ai_2026-06-26.csv`. The retained graph selects Spain and the pooled worldwide aggregate at SOC major-group level for May 2026, metric percentage. The same published data reuse statement applies.
- **USDOL/ETA, O*NET® 30.3 Database.** [Original occupation workbook](https://www.onetcenter.org/dl_files/database/db_30_3_excel/Occupation%20Data.xlsx). Descriptions are supplied in `data/input/jev/us_catalogue.csv`. [CC BY 4.0 database license](https://www.onetcenter.org/license_db.html) also permits use of earlier versions. O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA. The authors join, normalize, and construct occupational inputs; USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed, or tested these modifications.
- **US Bureau of Labor Statistics (2026), Artificial Intelligence exposure categories, 2025–35 projections.** [Provider and workbook](https://www.bls.gov/emp/publications/ai-exposure-categories.htm). Four categories used as an ordinal comparison, not a cardinal observed-exposure measure. [BLS copyright information](https://www.bls.gov/bls/linksite.htm) explains reuse of agency-produced material and exceptions for third-party content.
- **Felten, Edward, Manav Raj, and Robert Seamans (2023), How will Language Modelers like ChatGPT Affect Occupations and Industries?** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01157); [data repository](https://github.com/AIOE-Data/AIOE), workbook `Language Modeling AIOE and AIIE.xlsx`. The author-supplied workbook is archived here; language-modeling capability scores are joined through the fixed nearest crosswalk and ranked into percentiles. No separate broad redistribution license is asserted by this documentation.

## Adoption observations

- **ONTSI (2023), Uso de inteligencia artificial y big data en las empresas españolas.** [Report](https://www.ontsi.es/sites/ontsi/files/2023-02/Brujula_IA_Big_data_2023.pdf).
- **ONTSI (2024), Indicadores de uso de inteligencia artificial en las empresas españolas.** [Report page](https://www.ontsi.es/es/publicaciones/Indicadores-de-uso-de-inteligencia-artificial-en-las-empresas-espanolas-0).
- **Banco de España (2025), La adopción de la inteligencia artificial en las empresas españolas: un primer análisis basado en la EBAE.** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.53479/39705). November 2024 survey observation.
- **Funcas (2026), III Encuesta Funcas sobre Inteligencia Artificial.** [12 January 2026 release](https://www.funcas.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NOTAPRENSA-1.pdf). Frequent ChatGPT use by Spanish residents aged 18–75: 4%, 14%, 28% in 2023–25.

`spain_ai_adoption_timing_sources.csv` is a reconstruction of the retained published percentages, with original links and concepts. Annual firm values are 7.7%, 11.8%, 9.6%, 11.3% in 2021–24; the separate EBAE value is 19.9%. Dates locate observations on the manuscript's plot: annual observations use the specified plotting convention, and the November survey marker does not purport to identify an exact interview day. This file does not reproduce survey microdata or whole source reports.

## Author constructions and models

The cosine archive contains the exact 756 US and 502 Spanish `qwen3-embedding:4b` vectors from the author's original cache, preserving float64 precision and code order. Exposure assignment is reconstructed in `code/lib/cosine_replay.py`, including the military-officer correction. The supplied nearest crosswalk preserves semantic texts and matching diagnostics. English occupation labels are frozen author translations used only for presentation.

Jev uses `jev-1.13.0`, an exhaustive hierarchy across all 756 US categories, probability-weighted exposure, a distinct direct ten-level Score, and three task-bundling tiers. Exact questions/rubrics, raw responses, full distributions, inputs and source metadata are archived. The direct measure is a semantic prediction, not a new measurement of Spanish task-level AI usage. The tier rubric draws on [Garicano's 2026 task-bundling presentation](https://bcf.princeton.edu/events/luis-garicano-on-ai-and-messy-jobs/); operative wording is frozen in `questions.json` and `code/lib/jev_questions.py`. Repeating live requests may yield different vectors. Default replay makes no paid or authenticated service calls.

## Estimation software

`reghdfe` and `ftools` are by Sergio Correia, with MIT notices preserved. `sdid` and `sdid_event` are frozen Stata implementations associated with Clarke, Pailañir and Ciccia; source and GPL notices are preserved. `unique` and support helpers retain their original headers. Exact hashes are in `docs/stata_manifest.json`.

Continuous DiD uses Callaway, Goodman-Bacon and Sant'Anna's implementation. `renv.lock` records all R package versions and exact source commits: `contdid` `443c1c12f013bdaf19503d9d0a4f7a494e9eb22f`, `BMisc` `eb5164b7030bc8a53a0bed91425959cb75f41f97`, and `ptetools` `915cc31099cf2df158f09375f8e639379d99c9a0`. Source repositories and package-specific licenses are recorded there. No R or Stata executable is redistributed.
