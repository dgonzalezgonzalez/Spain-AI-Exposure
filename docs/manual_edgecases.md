# TEV crosswalk review

All 502 new automatic crosswalks were inspected by the Codex assistant against the frozen Spanish definitions, tasks and examples and actual available US descriptions. This is assistant-led source review, not independent human adjudication, a gold standard, or an accuracy evaluation. No Jev-era override list was applied automatically. The review selected codes without using exposures or econometric outcomes.

There are 56 explicit corrections. A retained match can still be a qualified or severely limited proxy. The complete occupation-by-occupation rationale, including unflagged cases and catalogue/source gaps, is frozen in `data/input/tev/author_review.json`. Correction count cannot be compared with the Jev release as an accuracy statistic.

## Correction rationales

- **0020:** The Spanish category includes all enlisted ranks, including infantry soldiers, not just shipboard sailors. Merchant-vessel navigation and cargo maintenance misrepresent this breadth. No military category is available; broad protective service is an explicitly approximate proxy.
- **1219:** The source describes specialized planning/administrative department heads and assistance to senior management, not the highest company-wide executive role. Correct the CEO match to an administrative-services management proxy; strategic planning and facilities functions remain broader than that proxy.
- **1312:** Source explicitly covers large-scale fishery/aquaculture production planning, contracts, budgets and markets. First-line worker supervision understates the managerial production role. Agricultural managers explicitly includes aquacultural operations; retain the capture-fishery component as a proxy limitation.
- **2122:** Source describes specialist nursing care planning, provision, supervision, education and research. It does not supply the medical diagnosis/testing role central to the selected Nurse Practitioner description. Correct to the broader Registered Nurses category, which explicitly covers nursing assessment/care plans and case management; the Spanish specialty distinction is not separately represented.
- **2323:** Private/academy music and dance instruction explicitly outside primary/secondary/higher education conflicts with the formal postsecondary teacher level. Correct to Self-Enrichment Teachers as a nonformal instruction proxy; occupational arts-training goals remain an imperfect component.
- **2324:** Private/academy drama/visual-art instruction explicitly outside formal education conflicts with the formal postsecondary teacher level. Correct to Self-Enrichment Teachers, retaining a possible occupational-training scope limitation.
- **2325:** Full task text covers user/organization IT training-needs analysis, materials, workshops and training evaluation. ## Retained limitations

Examples include subject-specific university teachers as a proxy for all university teachers; nursing assistants for home carers; raw-hide tanning versus finished-leather craft; pulp/paper manufacture versus downstream paper-goods conversion; electrical/electronic assembly versus repair; residual nonmechanical assembly versus machinery assembly; wild fishing versus aquaculture; and broad occupations spanning several US categories. These limitations remain explicit in the full review. No unobserved employment shares were invented.

CNO7199 has a frozen-source inconsistency: its supplied tasks repeat carpentry while its residual structural title/examples identify demolition, scaffolding, height work and drywall. This experiment preserves the source rather than silently changing it.

For flagged baseline mismatches, the automatic crosswalk reuses the already acquired top-24 TEV match. Unflagged cases initially retain the baseline match. The assistant review can correct either group. The matching weights used in the separate weighted exposure remain conditional on the fixed cosine shortlist; these review overrides do not alter the raw responses or their probabilities.
