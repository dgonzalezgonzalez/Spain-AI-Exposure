\section{Three Job Tiers}
\label{sec:v1_job_tiers}
Let $\tau_{jt}$ be Jev's probability for tier $t\in\{1,2,3\}$. The assigned tier and treatment indicators are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:v1_jev_tier_assignment}
\widehat T_j=\arg\max_t\tau_{jt},\qquad D_j^r=\mathbf{1}\{\widehat T_j=r\},\quad r=1,2.
\end{equation}
Probability vectors are normalized after API rounding; ties select the smallest tier number. Tier 3 is the omitted control group.
