Second, Online Appendix~\ref{sec:v1_job_tiers} examines tier 1 jobs with separable outputs relative to pooled strongly bundled and relational jobs (tiers 2 and 3), using the local TEV classification. The preferred within-CNO1 estimates show higher registered unemployment in tier 1 and fewer new contracts, although the later contract difference is imprecise. The full-sample pre-treatment joint tests reject for both unemployment and contracts ($p=0.010$ and $p=0.007$). Excluding 2021 leaves a rejection for unemployment ($p=0.005$) while the contract test does not reject ($p=0.226$). These diagnostics warrant interpreting the estimates as differences in occupational adjustment rather than evidence that stronger task bundling protects jobs.
