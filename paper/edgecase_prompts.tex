\subsubsection*{Local TEV matching and screening request}
The template below is instantiated once per occupation with the 24 highest-ranked cosine candidates, preserving their rank order and full descriptions. The three questions return independent judgments over the same state. The selected match is reused for the verified crosswalk only when the mismatch probability is at least 0.5. The other request groups the tier and direct-exposure rubrics reproduced in their respective subsections, using occupation evidence alone. Exact occupation-specific requests and responses are archived in the replication package.
